## Summary

The seventh test build of the aelrynth 2026.10.5.1 merge. Nothing has been tested in game.
Details: `2026-10-05-mini-remote-is-ilt-hub.md`.

## Changes

- **The tabbed ILT Hub window is gone; the Mini Remote is the Hub** (UtilityBelt style).
  - The new **V/H** button switches between stacked sections and three columns.
  - The new **Options** button (or right-click) opens the Hub's windows: Character, Quests, Pets,
    Banking, Gear and Games. Each is its own window.
- `/ra hub` and the dashboard's **Char** button now open the Mini Remote.
  - New command: `/ra hub open <section>`.
  - Char's right-click menu lists the section windows.
- Bank auto-refresh and the gear scans now run while the Mini Remote or any Hub window is open.

## Not yet ported from UtilityBelt's Mini Remote

- Storm and Reset Storm, the Guardian textbox, and the Fellow toggle.
- The LumS, KillL, PassL and Conv rate lines.
- The loot-profile and character-profile pickers.
