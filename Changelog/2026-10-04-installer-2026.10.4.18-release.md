## Changes

- **Floating HUDs.** Three new undockable windows: an item count HUD, a UB-style Mini Remote, and
  a quest favorites HUD. The item HUD and Mini Remote are set up from Inventory Management >
  **Inventory HUDs...** (both the ImGui Advanced Settings and the Avalonia Settings panel) or
  `/ra huds`. The ILT Hub quest tracker gained a favorites star, the floating favorites HUD and a
  **Pop out** button. See `2026-10-04-floating-huds.md`.
- **ILT Hub pet roster** (RynthAi 0.6.22, first shipped here). The Pet tab shows UB-style stats per
  pet, sorts, and summons by type (Combat, Healing, Cosmetic). See
  `2026-10-04-ilt-hub-pet-roster.md`.

## Upgrade notes

- No plugin API change. The engine's Inventory HUDs button needs RynthAi 0.6.23 or newer; older
  plugins ignore the command.
- `engine.json` may still be at `Trace` from earlier debugging; set it back to `Info` in the launcher.
