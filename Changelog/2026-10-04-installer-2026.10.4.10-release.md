# Installer 2026.10.4.10

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Branch:** `SK-local` (RynthCore `a7cdbee`, RynthSuite `898bf8b`)
**Previous release:** 2026.10.4.9

## Contents
- **Engine 2026.10.4.10:** the CreateObject hook records each object's name and ItemType from the
  server's create descriptor. Off-thread lookups therefore work at login, before the 500 ms
  identity snapshot can resolve names (`2026-10-04-create-seeds-and-fatal-only-crash-logger.md`).
- **RynthCore.SehTrampoline.dll v2:** `native-crash.log` now records fatal crashes only:
  - Unhandled exceptions are caught by a chained unhandled-exception filter.
  - Faults in engine/plugin code are logged immediately, because the runtime kills the process
    without running any filter.
  - Other first-chance AVs are written only if the process dies soon after.
  - The stack sweep stops at StackBase.
  - Per-thread guards stop the logger from logging its own faults and AVs that its `SEH_*`
    wrappers catch.
- **RynthAi 0.6.16:** keys, gems, spell components, food, misc items and portals are classified
  by ItemType and ownership instead of being left as "Unknown" and rechecked every 2 s
  (`RynthSuite/Changelog/RynthAi-0.6.16-typed-item-classification.md`). The CLASSIFY-GIVEUP
  flood at login is gone.
