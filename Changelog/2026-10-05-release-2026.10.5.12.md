# Release 2026.10.5.12

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Previous release:** 2026.10.5.11 (saved in `installer/previous-release`)

## Contents

- **RynthAi 0.5.14-legacy-ui:** pack items (recall gems, keys, spell components, food and misc) are filed as inventory again. They can be assigned to Mini Remote slots: no more "That item is no longer in your pack", and no more side pack placed in the slot instead of the item. Mini Remote assignment and every HUD chat message are now written to the Huds trace. See `2026-10-05-rynthai-0.5.14-pack-items-as-inventory.md`.
- Engine is unchanged from 2026.10.5.11 (native-inventory drag hook, Guardian vendor and item lookup).
