## Contents
- **RynthJuice 0.1.2:** mob health polling used to re-check the same first 80 of about 700
  tracked objects. It now reaches every tracked object (`RynthSuite/Changelog/RynthJuice-0.1.2-rotating-mob-poll.md`):
  - Candidates are checked in rotation, 80 per poll.
  - Confirmed attackable mobs are polled every cycle, so health bars stay continuous.
  - Objects with a known non-creature ItemType are never tracked.
  - The safety cap was raised from 800 to 4096.
- **Engine 2026.10.4.10 changes are unchanged** apart from the version stamp. This includes
  the create-time name/type seeds, which RynthJuice now relies on to drop items immediately.
  The engine is stamped 2026.10.4.11.
