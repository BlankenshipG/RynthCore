## Changes

- **Items window: Shields section.** "Add Selected Shield", the shield list with delete, and the
  auto-equip toggle. Shields no longer appear under Weapons. See
  `2026-10-04-items-panel-shields.md`.
- **RynthAi panel Map and Lua buttons work.** Map toggles the dungeon map and Lua toggles the
  Lua Scripts editor. Both are also available as `/ra map` and `/ra lua`. In Avalonia mode both
  windows are now drawn on top of the overlay. The dungeon map also reopens again after being
  closed with its X. See `2026-10-04-dashboard-map-and-lua-buttons.md`.

## Upgrade notes

- No plugin API change (still v69). RynthAi must be 0.6.20 or newer for the new Shields button and
  the new Map/Lua commands (this installer ships 0.6.21).
- `engine.json` may still be at `Trace` from earlier debugging; set it back to `Info` in the launcher.
