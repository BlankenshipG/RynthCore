## How it was deployed

- Engine: `scripts\Publish-EngineCoreClr.ps1 -EngineOnly -AllowBehind -MsBuildArgs -p:Version=2026.10.5.39`.
  `-AllowBehind` is needed because the script's guard checks a `status-export` branch, and this
  repo has no such branch. The engine it replaced (2026.10.5.38) was built from this same branch,
  one commit earlier, so nothing was rolled back.
- RynthAi: `dotnet publish -c Release` (NativeAOT, the same way the installer builds it), copied
  over the plugin DLL.
- Both injected clients (pid 48400 and 8396) hot-reloaded onto the new engine within about 20 s.
  Both staged the new RynthAi build, and their heartbeats were healthy afterwards. Client 48400 has
  now used its 3 hot reloads (`MaxHotReloads`): any newer engine needs that client restarted. The
  third acclient (pid 15208) isn't injected. New launches get the new files.

## Previous release (rollback)

The files that were replaced are saved in `C:\Games\RynthCore\HotBackups\20261012-before-2026.10.5.39-hot\`:

  2026.10.12.1 (an upstream build, not the one from the 2026.10.5.38 installer).

To roll back, copy the RynthAi DLL back first, then the engine pdb and then the engine DLL into
`Runtime\`. Running clients that still have a hot reload left reload onto it by themselves.
