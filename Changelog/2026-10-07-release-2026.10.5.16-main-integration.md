## Left out on purpose

ab18df2 on `SK-local`, "log CoreCLR-hosted engine AVs immediately in the native crash logger",
patches the crash logger rewrite in `native/SehTrampoline/SehTrampoline.c` from a7cdbee. `main`
never got a7cdbee: its trampoline is the older 406-line version, against 674 lines on `SK-local`,
so the cherry-pick conflicted in four places. a7cdbee also changes create-time object seeding,
which overlaps `main`'s own identity seeds from 4be686e. Bringing the crash logger over is its own
port, and it only takes effect after `scripts/Build-SehTrampoline.ps1` rebuilds the native DLL.
ab18df2 stays on `SK-local` until then.

5926a46 is the same heartbeat fix as 2c67875 on the `fix/restore-sk-features` line, so it was not
picked twice.

## Conflicts resolved
