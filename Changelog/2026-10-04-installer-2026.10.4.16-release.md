## Changes

- **Floating panels are destroyed on the thread that owns them.** `LayeredWindow.Dispose`
  destroys inline when the caller owns the window, and otherwise posts a self-destroy
  message to the panel window itself. Before, the destroy always went to the game
  thread, so panels created on another thread could be left frozen on screen.
- **The login-time pop-out waits for the game window hook**, so restored floating panels
  are created on AC's game thread like every other pop-out. It falls back after 60 s.

See `2026-10-04-panel-window-owner-thread-fixes.md` for details.
