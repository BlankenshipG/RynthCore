# Installer 2026.10.4.6 — RynthAi tool launch buttons

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Branch:** `feat/full-installer` (testing only — not merged to `main`)

## Changes
- Bundles **RynthAi 0.5.4**, which adds **Loot Editor** and **Monster Editor** launch buttons to the
  in-game Items window and the Advanced Settings → Looting page. No more launching the editors from
  the shell or Explorer while playing.
- No installer script changes; tool locations are unchanged (`<CoreDir>\Tools\LootEditor`,
  `<SuiteDir>\RynthAi\MonsterEditor`), and RynthAi finds them through the registered install paths.
