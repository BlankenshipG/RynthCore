## Included changes

- ILT Guardian window, ported from UB-IT's Guardian tab: Temple / Attribute guardian riddle
  translator (chat auto-detect and paste box), hand-in to the guardian with an optional vendor
  buy for the Temple guardian, and the attribute turn-in tracker (/myquests, falling back to
  /qb wait stamps). The last answer also shows on the Mini Remote with a Give button.
  `/ra guardian ...`.
- Mini Remote slots can now be filled by dragging from AC's own inventory (empty slots) or from
  a popped-out RynthCore Inventory. Clicking an item in the RynthCore Inventory selects it in the
  game, the remote remembers the last selected pack item for 2 minutes, and the Inventory's
  right-click menu has "Add to Mini Remote".

See `2026-10-05-guardian-and-remote-slots.md` for details and known limits.
