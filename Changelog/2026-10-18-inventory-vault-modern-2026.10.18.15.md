# Inventory: the Vault in the Modern look too (engine 2026.10.18.15)

On Aelrynth, the Modern Inventory's header has a Vault button (a treasure chest), left of the
Mini Remote, RynthSort, Item Count and Global Inventory buttons. Before, only the Classic look had
the Vault, and rynth's main is the same.

- **The view:** clicking the chest shows the account vault in place of the item grid, in the grid's own style. Each item has its icon and its stack count, and hovering shows its name.
- **Caption:** it says how full the vault is ("Aelrynth Vault: 12 of 50"), or the read's status before the first read, with a Refresh button.
- **Withdraw:** right-click an item, then Withdraw, the same as in the Classic look.
- **Back to the packs:** click the chest again, or pick a pack in the pack column.

Both looks share the caption, the empty-vault text and the Withdraw menu.
