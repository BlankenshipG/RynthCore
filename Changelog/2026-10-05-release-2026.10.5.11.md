## Included changes

- **Mini Remote: drags from AC's own inventory use the dragged item.** The engine hooks AC's
  drag start (`UIElementManager::StartDragandDrop`) instead of reading the selection, which AC
  leaves on the last clicked item. These drags can now go on any slot, including assigned ones.
  See `2026-10-05-native-drag-real-item.md`.
- **ILT Guardian hand-in finds the vendor and the item.** Vendors are recognised by the weenie
  vendor flag (nothing was ever classed Vendor, so the nearby vendor was never found), and the
  hand-in item is looked up in a live walk of your packs (a fresh purchase was missed).
  See `2026-10-05-guardian-vendor-and-item-lookup.md`.

## What to check in game

- Engine log at startup: `Compat: drag-start hook ready`.
- Drag an item from the game inventory onto a Mini Remote slot; the log shows
  `DragDropHooks: AC drag start item=0x...` with that item's id.
- Temple guardian answer with the item not carried: the IltHub log shows
  `[IltGuardian] vendor for '<item>': <vendor>`, then the buy and the give.
