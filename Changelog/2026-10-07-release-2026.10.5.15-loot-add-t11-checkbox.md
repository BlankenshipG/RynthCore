# RynthCore 2026.10.5.15 — "Include T11 attributes" in the Add to loot profile popup

**Date:** 2026-10-07
**Branch:** `feat/loot-add-t11-checkbox` (from `fix/restore-sk-features` 5926a46)
**Previous release:** 2026.10.5.14
**Pairs with:** RynthAi 0.5.21-legacy-ui (RynthSuite `feat/loot-add-t11-attributes`)

## What's new

The "Add to loot profile" popup has a new **Include T11 attributes** checkbox under Match. The
popup opens from the Inventory panel's right-click menu, or from "Add selected item" in the Loot
Editor.

- When checked, RynthAi adds the item's T11 conditions to the rule: tier, weapon grade,
  damage %, every modifier, slot special and Cast on Strike. Each must be at least as good as
  the clicked item's.
- It works with every match mode, and the preview updates as soon as it is toggled.
- The tooltip lists what is added, and why Can Wield and Zone Locked are not: they depend on
  the character and on where the item was assessed.
- "(not a T11 item)" shows next to the checkbox when the item isn't T11 gear.

## Compatibility

- `LootEditItemRequestDto.IncludeT11` is new; an older RynthAi ignores it.
- `LootEditItemDraftDto.IncludeT11` and `IsT11` are new; an older RynthAi never sends them, so
  they read false. With an older RynthAi the checkbox has no effect, and "(not a T11 item)"
  shows for every item.
