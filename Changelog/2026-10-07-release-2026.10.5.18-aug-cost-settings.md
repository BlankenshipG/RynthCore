# RynthCore engine 2026.10.5.18 — Aug cost settings in the Progression tab

**Date:** 2026-10-07
**Previous release:** 2026.10.5.17 (Progression tab laid out like UB's ILT Tools)
**Branch:** `QOL-items`
**Pairs with:** RynthAi 0.5.24-legacy-ui. With an older RynthAi the editor is hidden.

## What's new

The Augmentations section has a collapsible **Cost settings for <world>** block. Its header says
whether the world is on the InfiniteLeaftide defaults or has its own (custom) rules.

- **Tier multipliers:** five boxes (Below S1, From S1, From S2, From S3, From S4). The defaults are
  UB's 1 / 4 / 8 / 16 / 24, and decimals are allowed.
- **Per-aug rules:** a table with Base, Growth%, S1, S2, S3, S4, Cap (0 = no limit) and Coins per
  level.
- **Reset to InfiniteLeaftide defaults:** drops this world's changes.
- A note explains the formula: level i costs (Base + (i − 1) × Base × Growth% / 100) × the
  multiplier of its tier, and a tier starts at its S level.

Every box commits on Enter or when it loses focus, so a half-typed value never reaches RynthAi.
RynthAi saves the rules for the current world only, and the plan above re-prices on the next
snapshot.

## Data (`UI/Data/ProgressionData.cs`)

- `AugPlanner.Config`, of type `AugCostConfig`: World, Custom, Multipliers and Rows.
- `AugCostRule`: Key, Label, Base, Percent, Coins, S1 to S4, and Cap.
