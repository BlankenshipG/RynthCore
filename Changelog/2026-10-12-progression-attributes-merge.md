# Progression: one Attributes section (2026.10.5.44)

Skills > Progression merges the old **XP planner** and **Attribute raiser** into a single
**Attributes** section:

- Retail XP-table costs (next N raises) stay at the top.
- Below a separator, an **Infinite attributes** segment appears when RynthAi reports the
  server supports raising past the retail cap (`/xp` + `/attr`), including
  **Auto-raise past retail max**.

Pairs with RynthAi 0.5.53 (aug Refresh current fix). See
`RynthSuite/Changelog/2026-10-12-aug-refresh-current-and-attributes-merge.md`.
