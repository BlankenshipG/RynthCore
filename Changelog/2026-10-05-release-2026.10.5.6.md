## Summary

The fifth test build of the aelrynth 2026.10.5.1 merge. Nothing has been tested in game.
Details: `2026-10-05-floating-window-placement.md`.

## Fixes

- **Plugin windows open near the middle the first time.** This covers Pets, the popped-out Quests
  window, the Item HUD, the Mini Remote and the ILT Hub. Before, they opened in the top-left corner,
  hidden under popped-out panels.
- **Off-screen check.** Each time one of those windows is shown, it moves to the middle if less than
  40 px of it is on screen. The ILT Hub's rescue now uses the middle instead of (40,40).
- **Dungeon Map** opens centred when nothing is saved for it.
- **Engine panels open clear of popped-out windows.** A docked panel that would be more than half
  hidden opens at the nearest clearer spot. The move is saved and logged by `ImGuiPanelHost`.

## Build notes

- Only the usual warnings: Decal isn't installed on the build machine, so there's no Decal bridge;
  and CS0618 in `EntryPoint.cs`.
